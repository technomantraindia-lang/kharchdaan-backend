3. How to Make Modifications in Backend (Step-by-Step)

Whenever management needs to reorganize positions, move a leader, edit customer details, or approve KYC, follow these exact step-by-step procedures:

Modification 1: Moving a Member to a Different Position

ACTION 1 How to Move a Distributor to Another Leg or Parent
  1. Open MLM Management > Placement Tree (/admin/mlm/tree).
  2. Find the member you wish to move. Click their blue Info button.
  3. In the side drawer, click the orange Move Member button. (Or visit /admin/mlm/tree/move/{id} directly).
  4. In the New Parent search box, type the Name or Customer ID of the person you want them to sit under.
  5. Select an available slot: Left, Middle, or Right.
  6. Click Preview Movement. The system will check:
    • Is the slot open? (Yes/No)
    • Is there any loop? (Protected: you cannot place a parent under their own child).
  7. Click Confirm & Apply Move. The move is complete! The distributor and their entire downline team will instantly update on the live tree.

Modification 2: Editing a Member's Phone, Name, or Address

ACTION 2 How to Update Member Profile Information
  1. In the left sidebar, click MLM Management > Members (/admin/mlm/members).
  2. Use the search bar at the top to type the member's Customer ID, Name, or Mobile Number.
  3. Click the Actions > Edit button next to their name.
  4. Update their Name, Mobile, Email, or Residential Address (City, Pincode).
  5. To reset their password, simply type a new password in the password field.
  6. Click Update Member at the bottom.

Modification 3: Reviewing & Approving KYC Documents

ACTION 3 How to Approve Bank & PAN Details for Payouts
  1. Open MLM Management > Members.
  2. Filter the list by KYC Status > Pending.
  3. Click KYC Review next to the member.
  4. Verify that the Account Holder Name matches their legal PAN card name.
  5. Click View Cheque Image to confirm the IFSC code and Bank Account number.
  6. Click the green Approve KYC button. Their status changes to Approved and they become eligible for direct bank transfers during payout cycles!

4. How to Check Commissions & Process Payouts

Commission earnings are calculated automatically whenever a customer places an order on Kharchdaan.com:

The Direct Selling PV Formula Explained

📊
Active Formula: Commission = (Order Amount / 3000) × Level PV Rate × 20%
- Levels 0 to 7 (High Level): 13.5 PV points per unit.
- Levels 8 to 19 (Low Level): 0.75 PV points per unit.
- Commission income is distributed upline across all 20 levels in the hierarchy.

Step-by-Step: How to Run a Payout Cycle

PAYOUT STEP 1 Open the Payout Manager
Go to MLM Management > Payouts (/admin/mlm/payouts). You will see previous payout cycles, total commission paid, and pending balances.
PAYOUT STEP 2 Generate New Cycle
Click + Generate Payout Cycle. Choose the date period (e.g. 1st to 15th, or 1st to 30th). The system scans all completed orders during that period and sums up the exact earnings for each KYC-approved distributor.
PAYOUT STEP 3 Download Bank NEFT / IMPS Excel File
Inside the generated cycle, click Export Bank Sheet (Excel/CSV). You will get a clean spreadsheet containing:
  • Beneficiary Name
  • Bank Account Number
  • IFSC Code & Bank Name
  • Net Payable Rupee Amount (after TDS deduction)
Upload this file directly into your corporate net-banking portal (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, etc.) to disburse payments.
PAYOUT STEP 4 Upload Payment Proof & Mark as Paid
Once the bank transfers are complete, upload the bank UTR confirmation receipt and click Mark Cycle as Paid. The system records the ledger entries, updates distributor wallets, and sends payment confirmations.
