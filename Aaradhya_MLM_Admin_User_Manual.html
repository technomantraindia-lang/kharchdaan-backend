1. System Architecture & Fundamental Rules

This manual enables your administrative team to manage all MLM operations directly from the web backend dashboard without technical programming knowledge.

💡
Key Concept: Placement Tree vs. Sponsor Network
In this enterprise direct-selling system, there are two distinct networks:
  • Placement Tree (1:3 Matrix): Determines physical hierarchy on the visual chart. Every single member card can have strictly at most 3 direct children (Left, Middle, Right).
  • Sponsor Network (Direct Referrals): Tracks who brought in whom. A distributor can personally refer unlimited people. If their 3 placement slots are full, new referrals spill over downline into open slots.

2. Step-by-Step: Using the Visual Placement Tree

The interactive tree canvas allows you to explore, inspect, and drill down into any distributor's 1:3 ternary network across all 20 levels.

1
Access the Placement Tree
From your left admin sidebar, click MLM Management > Placement Tree or open /admin/mlm/tree. The page automatically mounts with Top ID Nimesh Jagirdar and opens Level 1 and Level 2 automatically.
2
Use View Mode Toggles (Cards / Compact / Circles)
In the upper-left toolbar, switch between display modes:
  • Cards: Shows full details (Name, Customer ID, Phone, Level, Sponsor, Total Income).
  • Compact: Narrower cards allowing 3x more nodes on screen at once.
  • Circles: Circular nodes for high-density bird's-eye views.
3
Instantly Jump to Any Member Using Search
Type any Customer ID (e.g., C22030018), Name (e.g., Gaurav), or Mobile Number into the Search & Jump input box. Click Find, then click on the search result to automatically scroll and zoom directly to that member card.
4
Quick Leader Network Toolbar
Above the tree canvas, click any orange leader pill (e.g. Janakben Bihola, Niruben Zala, Gaurav Agarwal, or Devang Raval) to instantly jump directly to their organization branch.
5
Expand / Collapse Branches Dynamically
Click the circular + / - button at the bottom of any card to load or hide their 3 placement children. You can also click toolbar buttons:
  • L1: Shows Level 1 leaders.
  • L2: Shows Levels 1 and 2.
  • L3: Shows all teams down to Level 3.
  • Expand All: Expands the entire visible canvas.
  • Collapse: Collapses back to the root card.
6
Inspect Complete Member Profile Drawer
Click the blue Info button on any card. A side drawer slides out from the right displaying:
  • Total Commission Income calculated from all store orders.
  • Direct Sponsor name and phone number.
  • Placement Parent name and slot.
  • KYC Status (Approved/Pending) and full shipping address.
7
Subtree Focus Drill-Down
Click the orange Focus button on any card to isolate that distributor as the temporary root. A breadcrumb bar appears at the top (e.g., Top Root > Gaurav Agarwal > Devang Raval). Click Back to Top Root anytime to return to Nimesh Jagirdar.

Zoom & Canvas Navigation Tips:

3. Step-by-Step: Moving a Member to a New Position

When the client or management wants to reorganize branches, change who sits under whom, or move a distributor to another leg, use the built-in Move Member tool.

ℹ️
What happens when you move a member?
When a member is moved to a new parent and slot:
  • Entire Downline Travels Together: All children, grandchildren, and teams under the moved member remain intact and move with them automatically.
  • Sponsor Tree Unaffected: Direct referral commissions and sponsor relationships do NOT change unless you explicitly choose to change the sponsor.
  • Automatic Loop Prevention: The system strictly blocks circular movements (e.g. you cannot place a parent under their own downline child).

Detailed Step-by-Step Move Procedure:

1
Open the Move Tool
Two easy ways to open the move form:
  • From Tree View: Open the Info drawer for the member you wish to move, and click the Move Member action button.
  • From Member List: Go to /admin/mlm/members, find the member, click the Actions dropdown, and select Move Position.
  • Direct URL: Navigate to /admin/mlm/tree/move/{MEMBER_ID}.
2
Review Current Position Snapshot
The top card displays the member's current state: Customer ID, Name, Current Placement Parent, Current Position (Left, Middle, Right), and total downlines being moved.
3
Select the New Placement Parent
In the New Placement Parent search box:
  • Type the Customer ID or Name of the distributor under whom you want to place this member.
  • Select the distributor from the dropdown search results.
4
Select an Available Placement Slot
The form will check the new parent's current children and show the 3 slots:
  • Left Slot
  • Middle Slot
  • Right Slot
Choose an available (open) slot. If a slot is already taken, the system will prevent duplicate assignment.
5
Click "Preview Movement"
Always click Preview Movement before saving. The system performs automatic sanity checks:
  • Verifies that the new slot is not occupied.
  • Verifies there is no circular loop.
  • Calculates new matrix levels and downline totals.
6
Confirm and Apply Movement
Click Confirm & Apply Move. The database updates atomically inside a transaction, the visual placement tree re-indexes, and you will be redirected back to the tree view with the member successfully in their new position.

4. Step-by-Step: Member Management & KYC Verification

How to register new distributors, update personal information, review legal compliance documents, and manage status.

Adding a New Distributor Manually

1
Navigate to Add Member
Click MLM Management > Members, then click the blue + Add New Member button (or visit /admin/mlm/members/create).
2
Fill Personal & Contact Details
Enter Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, Password, and Full Residential Address (City, Pincode, State).
3
Assign Sponsor & Placement Position
  • Direct Sponsor ID: Enter the Customer Code of the distributor who introduced them (e.g. C22020008).
  • Placement Parent ID: Enter the Customer Code of the node directly above them in the 1:3 tree.
  • Placement Position: Select Left, Middle, or Right.
4
Save & Generate Customer Code
Click Save Member. The system automatically creates their user account, assigns the Customer/Distributor role, generates their unique customer code, and adds them to the tree.

Reviewing and Approving KYC Documents

Before commissions can be paid out, distributors must submit verified KYC data:

Requirement What to Check Action
PAN Card 10-digit alphanumeric PAN format (e.g. ABCDE1234F). Matches member legal name. Encrypted in database; verified for TDS compliance.
Aadhaar Number 12-digit UID reference or front/back identity photo. Stored securely with hash verification.
Bank Account Account Number, Account Holder Name, IFSC Code, Bank Name and Branch. Must match name on PAN card for payout NEFT/IMPS.
Cancelled Cheque Image proof of cheque or bank passbook showing printed name and IFSC. Click View Cheque Image in admin to verify.
✅
How to Approve or Reject KYC:
1. Go to /admin/mlm/members/{id}/kyc.
2. Review the submitted documents and bank details.
3. Click Approve KYC to enable payouts, or click Reject KYC and enter a reason (e.g. "IFSC code mismatch") so the distributor can re-upload.

5. Step-by-Step: MLM Calculation Rules & Payout Cycles

How commission formulas calculate income from customer purchases and how payouts are processed.

Direct Selling Commission Calculation Formula

Active Calculation Rule: Direct Selling PV Formula v1

When an order is completed, commission income is distributed across 20 upline levels based on the product purchase amount:

Commission = (Order Total / PV Divisor) × Level PV Rate × Income Rate
- PV Divisor: 3,000
- High Level Rate (Levels 0 to 7): 13.5 PV
- Low Level Rate (Levels 8 to 19): 0.75 PV
- Income Rate: 0.20 (20%)

Modifying Calculation Rules (If Policy Changes)

1
Open Calculation Rules Module
Go to MLM Management > Calculation Rules (/admin/mlm/calculations).
2
Use the Commission Preview Simulator
Before making changes, enter a test order amount (e.g. ₹1,000 or ₹3,000) in the Simulator box. The table shows the exact rupee commission paid to each level from Level 0 to Level 19.
3
Create or Update Rule Version
To change percentages, click Create New Calculation Rule. Enter the new rates, set the effective date, and mark it active. Old rules are preserved for historical audit trails.

Running Payout Cycles & Processing Bank Transfers

1
Open Payout Cycles
Navigate to MLM Management > Payouts (/admin/mlm/payouts).
2
Generate New Payout Cycle
Click + Generate Payout Cycle. Select the period (e.g. Weekly: 1st to 7th, or Monthly: 1st to 30th). The system scans all eligible orders, computes net earnings minus TDS and admin fees, and generates the line items for all KYC-approved distributors.
3
Review, Approve & Export Bank NEFT Sheet
Review total payable amount. Click Export Excel / CSV to get the bank transfer sheet with Account Numbers, Beneficiary Names, and IFSC codes for bulk NEFT upload in your corporate bank portal.
4
Upload Proof & Mark Paid
After bank execution, upload the bank transaction UTR receipt / payment proof and click Mark as Paid. SMS/Email notifications are sent to distributors and wallet balances update automatically.

6. Frequently Asked Questions & Support

Q1: Can a distributor have 4 or more children directly under their card?

Answer: No. In accordance with the company's 1:3 placement matrix policy and database integrity constraints, every card has strictly up to 3 slots: Left, Middle, and Right. Any additional referrals sponsored by that leader must be placed downline under their team members as spillover.

Q2: What happens if a distributor wants to move their position?

Answer: An admin can use the Move Member tool (/admin/mlm/tree/move/{id}). Select an open slot under the desired parent and click Confirm & Apply Move. All downline members under that person will move with them automatically without breaking any team connections.

Q3: How do I change an admin or distributor password?

Answer: Go to MLM Management > Members, find the distributor, click Edit, enter the new password in the password field, and click Update Member.

Q4: Why was a member's payout placed on hold?

Answer: Payouts are automatically held if the distributor's KYC status is Pending or Rejected, or if their bank IFSC code is invalid. Once you approve their KYC in /admin/mlm/members/{id}/kyc, their held balance automatically rolls over into the next payout cycle.

Q5: How can we re-import the master Excel sheet if a new batch arrives?

Answer: Place the new Excel file in backend/excel sheetr/Aaradhya Gruh Udyog - Master Data.xlsx, and run the automated command: php artisan mlm:import-excel. It parses customers, validates 1:3 placement slots, updates transactions, and syncs the database.
